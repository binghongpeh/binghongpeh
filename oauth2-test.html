OAuth2 Diagnostic Tester

Test your OAuth2 credentials step-by-step. All requests go directly from your browser to your provider — nothing is stored or sent to any third party.

Provider & Shared Settings

Uses your existing refresh token to get a new access token. This is the fastest way to verify your OAuth2 setup is working.

Note: The Resource Owner Password Credentials (ROPC) grant sends your email and password directly to the token endpoint. Microsoft has deprecated ROPC for personal accounts and accounts with MFA. It works for Azure AD work/school accounts without MFA. Google and GitHub do not support this flow at all.
